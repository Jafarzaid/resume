01About

Most of my job is catching delays early, getting the right people on them, and changing the plan before customers notice.

I work on track-and-trace for Amazon shipments across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Türkiye and South Africa, in a 24/7 operation. A lot of that work was manual, so I started automating it with VBA macros, a browser extension and AI-assisted scripts. They now save about 1,100 hours a year for around 40 people.

Before this I spent three years on the Alexa team doing Arabic translation and QA. I'm currently doing an MBA in Operations & Supply Chain at Jamia Hamdard.
